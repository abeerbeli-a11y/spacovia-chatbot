# Business Info

Pulled from spacovia.com. Edit/correct anything that's off — the chatbot uses this as its knowledge base.

## Business name
Spacovia — white-label coworking software, made by Wepronex (a software company in Pakistan).

## What we do / sell
Spacovia runs room bookings, day passes, visitors, parcels, complaints and payments for coworking spaces — rebranded under the operator's own name, logo and colours (e.g. members open "app.yourspace.com").

Key features by audience:
- **Members** (client companies, their teams, walk-in guests): book meeting rooms/event space by the hour with price shown first, monthly room credits per company, day/night/week passes with no membership needed, raise and track complaints, see partner perks and announcements, install to home screen like an app with notifications.
- **Reception / front desk**: QR check-in for visitors (with WhatsApp to the host), parcel/delivery logging, on-the-spot room booking for guests, recording payments, approving/declining room requests, per-branch day/night shifts.
- **Managers/owners**: all branches and client companies in one view, payments verified against the bank before being counted, complaints routed to the right department (IT, housekeeping, accounts), staff check-in/out with location + live selfie, read-only accountant view with spreadsheet exports, configurable rules (hours, closed days, daily room limits).

Branding: each operator gets their own copy of the app under their own name/logo/colours/web address, their own database (fully separated from other operators), and emails sent from their own address. No app store download needed — works in the phone browser, added to the home screen.

## Common questions and answers
- **Do members need to download an app?** No — it opens in any phone browser and can be added to the home screen, working like an app with notifications. Nothing to download from an app store.
- **Will members see the Spacovia name?** No — every operator's copy is branded entirely under their own name, logo, and colours. Members never see "Spacovia."
- **Is my data kept apart from other operators?** Yes — every operator has their own separate database. No other operator can see your members, bookings, or payments.
- **I have more than one branch — is that one copy?** Yes — all locations run in one copy, with each front desk seeing only its own branch. Additional locations are free for the first month, then $49/month.
- **Who is behind Spacovia?** Wepronex, a software company in Pakistan. Spacovia was built inside a real, working coworking business, so it's built from how spaces actually run day to day.
- **How do I get started?** Book a demo — tell them the space's name and number of locations, and they'll show Spacovia running with the operator's own logo and colours already in it. Contact: hello@spacovia.com
